Algorithm

    Each open cell joins the queue at most once and checks four neighbours, so a full search costs O(V + E). It finds the shortest path because the queue only ever holds cells at two neighbouring distances, d and d + 1. As a result, the first time E comes off the queue, nothing closer is left to try.
